01 PROJECT OVERVIEW

A place for conversations to grow.

LivSocial’s desktop artwork presents a central content feed alongside a personal social circle and trending conversations. Navigation separates the home feed, communities, businesses, and profile areas. The result is a view of a social product organized around both people and the content they share.

03 DESIGN DIRECTION

Its own identity.
In every detail.

Social & community

Mustard yellow establishes the product identity, while white content panels keep photo posts and conversations readable. A persistent desktop navigation bar and supporting side columns give the main feed a clear position within a much broader community experience.
