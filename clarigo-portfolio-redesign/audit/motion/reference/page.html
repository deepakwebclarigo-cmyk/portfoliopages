FAQ

Questions we hear most

The short answers on what we build, how fast we ship, and how to get started.

What does Clarigo Infotech do?

+

Clarigo Infotech is an AI-first software and app development company. We design, build, and run AI agents, multi-agent systems, AI automation, voice bots and chatbots, custom web and mobile apps, blockchain solutions, and enterprise software, all with one in-house team.

Why choose Clarigo Infotech as your AI development company?

+

We have 10+ years of software delivery behind us and 60+ AI and software projects shipped. One team covers design, engineering, and long-term support, we take products from idea to a production MVP in 25 to 30 days, and we build on enterprise-grade, secure architecture that scales after launch.

Does Clarigo Infotech build mobile and web apps?

+

Yes. Alongside AI work we build custom web applications, mobile apps, SaaS platforms, and internal tools using React, Next.js, Node.js, Python, and Flutter, deployed on cloud infrastructure with Docker and Kubernetes.

How long does it take to build an MVP with Clarigo?

+

Most MVPs go from idea to a live, production-ready product in 25 to 30 days: discovery and clickable design in week one, core build in weeks two and three, then testing, launch, and handover. The same team keeps scaling it after launch.

Which industries does Clarigo Infotech work with?

+

Our deepest expertise is healthcare and logistics. We also build AI and software for manufacturing, finance, construction, education, retail, automobile, hospitality, real estate, agriculture, and government.

Can Clarigo build HIPAA-compliant healthcare software?

+

Yes. Our HIPAA-trained team builds healthcare software to HIPAA requirements, with HL7 and FHIR integrations into EHR and EMR systems. We also offer ready-made products, Clarigo Clinic OS for front-office automation and Clarigo EHR for AI-native records and practice management.

Which AI models and technologies does Clarigo use?

+

We work across OpenAI, Claude, and Gemini models, agent frameworks such as LangGraph, CrewAI, and MCP, automation platforms like n8n and Make, vector databases such as Pinecone and Qdrant, and cloud AI on Azure AI, AWS Bedrock, and Google Vertex AI.

Where is Clarigo Infotech located?

+

Our headquarters is in Vijay Nagar, Indore, India, and we serve clients in 20+ countries, working remotely across time zones.

How much does it cost to build an AI app or MVP?

+

It depends on scope, integrations, and the AI features involved. Clarigo Infotech gives a clear, scoped quote after a free AI consultation, and because most MVPs go live in 25 to 30 days with a senior-only team, the budget stays predictable instead of stretching over months.

Does Clarigo Infotech work with clients outside India?

+

Yes. Clarigo Infotech is headquartered in Indore, India, and builds AI and software for clients in 20+ countries, including the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, and Australia, working remotely across time zones.

Who owns the source code Clarigo builds?

+

You do. Every project comes with full source-code ownership: clean, documented code deployed on AWS, Azure, or your own infrastructure, with no lock-in.

Does Clarigo Infotech provide support after launch?

+

Yes. The same engineers who build your product stay on to monitor, improve, and scale it after launch, with dedicated 24/7 support coverage.

How do I start a project with Clarigo Infotech?

+

Book a free AI consultation through our contact page or email project@clarigoinfotech.com. We reply within one working day with next steps and a scoped plan.
