01 PROJECT OVERVIEW

A visual gateway to digital collections.

StreamDexa’s website screens present a digital-asset marketplace through collection grids, individual asset information, and profile-style pages. A dark promotional identity introduces the product, while lighter browsing layouts give each collection room to stand out. Creator-oriented headers and structured asset records connect discovery with a closer look at the listed material.

03 PRODUCT JOURNEY

How StreamDexa works.

The available interfaces show a progression from a collection catalog to the details of an individual asset.

  1. 01

    Browse the collection

    Explore artwork-led grids that place multiple digital items within a consistent catalog layout.

  2. 02

    Inspect an asset

    Move to a detailed presentation pairing the selected artwork with its displayed information and related items.

  3. 03

    Explore the creator

    Profile-style pages group collection content beneath a recognizable creator header and supporting navigation.

04 DESIGN CHALLENGE & SOLUTION

Give visual discovery a structured foundation.

Artwork needs space to stand out, but collection browsing also depends on clear labels and context. The screen set balances image-led discovery with quieter information panels.

  • Artwork first

    Consistent image grids let collections remain the strongest visual element during browsing.

  • Details alongside the image

    The asset layout keeps structured information near the artwork it describes.

  • A consistent catalog frame

    Shared headers and repeated content modules connect collection, profile, and registration pages.

05 DESIGN DIRECTION

Its own identity.
In every detail.
