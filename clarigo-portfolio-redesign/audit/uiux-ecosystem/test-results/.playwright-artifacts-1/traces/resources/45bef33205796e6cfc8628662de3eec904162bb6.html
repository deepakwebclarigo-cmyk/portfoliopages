04 DESIGN CHALLENGE & SOLUTION

Make a personal check-in visually approachable.

Reflective information can feel dense when every input is presented as a form. Heartsync uses expressive choices and color-coded indicators to create different visual entry points within a dark interface.

  • Expression before detail

    Emotion-style icons introduce a more personal alternative to a text-only opening.

  • Indicators with visual distinction

    Separate colors help distinguish the labeled sliders shown in the workspace.

  • Prompts in smaller cards

    Rounded boundaries break reflective content into manageable sections.

05 DESIGN DIRECTION

Its own identity.
In every detail.

Relationships & reflection

Deep purple and near-black surfaces support bright pink, orange, and green highlights. Emoji-like choices and colorful sliders introduce expression into the interface, while rounded cards divide the more detailed reflective content.
