# Calivora

Personal health and care companion. New identity created with built-in imagegen, transparent-background mode.

## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one premium modern logo for a personal health and care companion mobile app named Calivora. Exact wordmark: 'Calivora' (C a l i v o r a). A distinctive calm abstract C emblem made from two softly interlaced rounded leaf-like ribbons, suggesting continuity, personal care, and a gentle connection; balanced open negative space, minimal sophisticated geometry, strong small-icon readability. Emerald/teal #0FB98A with a restrained lighter accent #23CC9D. Below the symbol place Calivora in a clean rounded geometric sans serif, medium-semi-bold, deep plum #201331, carefully spaced. Centered stacked lockup, symbol above wordmark with a clear clean gap between the symbol and text so each can be used separately. Square composition, symbol around 42% canvas width, wordmark around 68%, logo occupying central 65% height with generous transparent margins. Genuine fully transparent alpha background. Flat crisp vector-like aesthetic, no shadows, no mockup, no tile, no checkerboard artwork, no tagline, no extra text, no medical cross, no caduceus, no heart clipart, no 3D effects. A premium approachable identity that harmonizes with a dark plum healthcare interface with emerald action buttons.

## Files

Primary and inverse stacked logos and the emblem are provided as genuine transparent 1000 x 1000 PNGs. Public app icons and horizontal lockups derive from the same generated artwork. Uploaded screenshots remain unchanged outside documented branding regions. No new real domain is asserted by the renamed registration and account labels.

Alpha edges were cleaned with a median mask and soft antialiasing before scaling; the original generated artwork is retained in the audit folder.
