# Strivaro — logo design brief

New portfolio name: **Strivaro**. Fitness goals, personal progress, virtual rewards, and community updates define the supplied interface.

The logo was created using the built-in imagegen tool. The primary PNG has a plum wordmark for light backgrounds; the inverse version uses a white wordmark for the supplied dark app interface. Both versions preserve the colored symbol and genuine alpha transparency. Each exported logo is exactly 1000 × 1000 pixels. The symbol-only transparent PNG is also provided.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Design one premium modern fitness mobile app logo for the new brand Strivaro. This is a final production transparent PNG identity, square composition, not a mockup. A distinctive compact abstract S made of two dynamic interlocking forward-moving ribbons: warm electric orange to amber upper ribbon and vivid cyan to cobalt lower ribbon. Suggest athletic momentum and a subtle upward progress tick through clean negative space; refined, balanced geometric construction, crisp vector-like edges, restrained gradient, flat frontal presentation. Below the symbol place the exact wordmark 'Strivaro' (S t r i v a r o) in a premium rounded geometric sans serif, semibold, deep plum #27173F, clean even kerning. Stacked centered symbol above wordmark. Symbol approximately 45% of canvas width, wordmark approximately 68%; compact lockup occupying central 70% height, ample transparent margins, readable at small sizes. Palette must harmonize with a dark purple fitness app with orange-red primary buttons and cyan-blue secondary actions. Background must be fully genuinely transparent alpha. No background, no tile, no checkerboard pixels, no slogan, no extra text, no shadows, no border, no realistic runner, no 3D rendering.
