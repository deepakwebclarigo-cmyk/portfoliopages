# AxoraBid — logo design brief

AxoraBid is the new name for the supplied automotive-auction portfolio. Seller/dealer entry, vehicle listings, media checklists, draft records, and auction views define the visible product.

The logo was created with the built-in imagegen tool. The primary color lockup and inverse white lockup each have genuine transparent alpha and an exact 1000 × 1000 pixel canvas. A separate transparent symbol-only PNG is also included. The inverse version is used on the existing blue app surfaces; the primary color version is used on white.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one final production logo for a premium automotive auction mobile platform named AxoraBid. Exact wordmark: 'AxoraBid' (A x o r a B i d). A clean distinctive geometric A emblem on the left, formed from two flowing road-like strokes with a subtle vehicle roofline or forward auction arrow in its negative space. Refined solid-vector aesthetic, balanced construction, smooth crisp edges, flat frontal view, strong small-size legibility. Royal blue #3158FF emblem with an understated deeper blue accent; wordmark deep navy #18285E in a modern geometric sans serif, medium-semi-bold weight, precise kerning. Single horizontal lockup: emblem left, AxoraBid wordmark right. Center the entire lockup on a square transparent canvas, occupying about 82% width and 24% height, generous transparent margins. No background, genuine transparent alpha, no tile, no checkerboard artwork, no mockup, no shadows, no slogan, no extra text, no car photograph, no gavel clip art, no 3D effect. Premium automotive technology identity; energetic but restrained.

## Implementation

All eleven supplied originals remain unchanged. The portfolio gallery includes ten interface designs and the rebranded identity tile. Published copies use precise branding-region edits, including the faint rotated onboarding name. The background beneath that watermark was reconstructed while preserving the car foreground; visible pixels outside the branding regions were verified unchanged. Content describes the visible interfaces without inventing the mobile framework, auction engine, verification provider, or commercial results.
